Privacy & cookies

Last updated November 26, 2026

Your music never leaves your device. Tracks has no accounts and no upload, so your projects, recordings and sounds are never sent to us. Google Analytics and Google ads load only if you choose Allow in the privacy notice.

This page covers freetracks.app and synth.freetracks.app. Tracks is made by JFound. Questions about this page or your data: support@ocari.co.

Your music stays on your device

The studio and the synth run entirely in your browser. Everything you make is saved in your browser's own storage on your device, not on a server:

  • Local storage: your current studio project (tracks.session.v1), songs you keep in this browser (tracks.songs.v1), studio settings (tracks.settings.v1), whether you closed the first-visit tip (tracks.tip.v1), and the synth's sound, saved patches and sequencer pattern (synth.session.v1, synth.patches.v1, synth.seq.v1).
  • IndexedDB: samples and SoundFonts you load (tracks-samples, tracks-soundfonts).

Microphone recording and MIDI keyboards are handled in your browser too. The browser asks your permission first, and the sound never leaves your device. Saving a project or exporting audio downloads a file to your computer. We cannot see, back up or recover any of this. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it.

The privacy notice: Allow or Decline

On your first visit a small notice asks whether to allow Google Analytics and ads. Nothing is sent to Google until you answer, and nothing is sent at all if you decline. Your answer is saved in this browser's local storage (jfound_cookie_consent) so the notice doesn't ask again.

To change your answer at any time, use Cookie settings. The link is at the bottom of every page and in the studio's Settings panel. It opens the notice again.

If you choose Allow

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-STDK4QN3F6) to count visits and see which pages and features people use. It is loaded through Cloudflare Zaraz, which keeps a random visitor ID and the visit details in its own cookies on freetracks.app (listed below) instead of Google's usual _ga cookie. Google keeps this analytics data for 14 months. Google's own explanation: How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Google AdSense

Ads help keep Tracks free. After you allow, the Google AdSense script loads and Google may show ads. Google and its partners may set or read their own advertising cookies to show ads and measure them, including ads based on your earlier visits to this and other sites. Google explains these cookies here: How Google uses cookies in advertising. You can turn off personalised ads at Google's My Ad Center.

If you choose Decline, neither Google Analytics nor the AdSense script is loaded, and Google is not contacted from our pages.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles your IP address and basic request details (such as your browser type) to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. Cloudflare also loads its Web Analytics script on every page, before and regardless of your choice. It counts page views and measures page speed, sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Community board

The community board runs on GitHub Discussions. Posting there means signing in to GitHub, and GitHub's own privacy statement and terms apply. We don't store anything about you on our side.

Changes to this page

If what Tracks stores or sends changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
