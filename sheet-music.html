Sheet music editor

Edit a score in the ABC format YuE2 reads and writes. The sheet music and the text stay in step: click a note to pick it, change it with the buttons or the keyboard, or type in the text. Everything stays in your browser.

The studio needs a computer or tablet. Save the score here and open it in the studio there.

Score check

    Lyrics

    YuE2 takes lyrics separately from the score, so they're saved as their own text file. Mark each part with a tag like [Verse] or [Chorus].

    Keys

    With the sheet music picked (not the text): ← → move between notes · ↑ ↓ a step · Shift+↑ ↓ an octave · Alt+↑ ↓ a semitone · 1–5 sixteenth to whole note · . dotted · R rest · Delete · I insert after · | add bar · Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Shift+Z redo.

    The buttons change the text so that every bar stays the right length and the score stays in YuE2's format. Open a score with Open .abc, or drop an .abc file on the page. The YuE2 guide shows how to use the score you save.
